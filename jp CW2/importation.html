Direct Importation

JP Motors can guide you through the key stages involved in sourcing and importing a vehicle to Uganda. Costs and timelines depend on the vehicle and shipping arrangements.

Import Process Overview

  • Discuss your preferred make, model, year and budget.
  • Source suitable vehicles from available markets and auctions.
  • Review vehicle details, condition information and shipping costs.
  • Coordinate freight and clearing arrangements with relevant providers.
  • Arrange delivery and applicable registration steps in Uganda.

Import With Clarity

Before proceeding, request a written cost breakdown covering the vehicle, freight, insurance, taxes, clearing, transport and registration where applicable.
